    Privacy Policy & Data Protection

    Pursuant to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023, Act No. 22 of 2023)

    1. Scope & Legal Framework

    This Privacy Policy outlines how LegalAI collects, processes, and protects digital personal data in strict alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the Information Technology Rules, 2021. This policy applies to all citizens, clients, advocates, and judicial researchers accessing our services.

    2. Privileged Client - Advocate Communications

    Under Section 126 and Section 129 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (and Section 132 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023), consultations booked between clients and enrolled advocates constitute privileged communications. LegalAI enforces encrypted session boundaries ensuring consultation briefs and client disclosures are accessible only to the designated advocate chambers.

    3. Lawful Basis & Explicit Consent

    We process personal data solely on lawful grounds under Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023:
    • Consent: Freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent obtained at the point of account registration and consultation booking.
    • Legitimate Uses: Compliance with judicial summons, statutory verification of Bar Council enrollment numbers, and prevention of fraudulent claims.

    4. Data Minimization & Zero Retention of AI Queries

    Legal query drafts submitted to our Hybrid-RAG assistant are processed in ephemeral memory for the duration of statutory retrieval and are not utilized to train public machine learning weights without explicit enterprise consent.

    5. Data Principal Rights & Grievance Redressal

    Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023, you hold the right to:
    • Access summaries of personal data processed by LegalAI.
    • Correction, completion, and updating of profile information.
    • Erasure of consultation history and account records upon settlement of active proceedings.
    • Nominate an authorized representative in the event of death or incapacity.

    For data inquiries or statutory grievances, contact our designated Grievance Officer: privacy@legalai.in.
